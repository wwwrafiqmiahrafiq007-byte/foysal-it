# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a professional, respectful, inclusive and constructive project community.

Expected behavior includes:

- Respectful technical discussion.
- Clear, reproducible bug reports.
- Evidence-based code review.
- Respect for different experience levels and viewpoints.
- No harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Do not publish private information, credentials, malicious content, abusive comments, or intentionally disruptive changes.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` and do not open a public issue. For community conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers through the repository's configured contact channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
